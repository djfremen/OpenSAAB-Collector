Adds the official OpenSAAB logo and Windows icon, plus a persistent Donate · Support OpenSAAB button. Donations are optional, unlock no features, and receive no capture or vehicle data. The donation link is blocked while capture/upload is active.

Compiled and visually checked on the EliteBook running Windows 10: logo, footer and optional donation dialog verified. Capture/upload engine is unchanged from 0.5.0, whose Chipsoft capture and private R2 upload were tested. No new adapter-compatibility claim is made by this branding release. Windows 8.1/Mongoose remains untested.
